/ GOOD TO KNOW

Clear terms before campaigns.

Is GameLaunch Bridge affiliated with Rockstar?

No. This is an independent marketing-services pilot, not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Do you already represent creators?

Not by default. We research potential candidates and confirm suitability, commercial interest and availability before suggesting a booking.

Are views or sales guaranteed?

No. Performance estimates are hypotheses, and any agreed minimum deliverables must be specifically documented.

Can brands use game footage?

Only with appropriate permissions and platform compliance. Our default concepts emphasize original product content rather than unlicensed game assets.
