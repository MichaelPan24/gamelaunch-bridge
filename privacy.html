Privacy notice

Last updated: 11 October 2026.

GameLaunch Bridge is the working name for an independently operated creator-marketing pilot, not an official GTA VI service. This notice describes how inquiries made through our public website currently work.

Who to contact

The current contact for the GameLaunch Bridge pilot is 1102847670py@gmail.com. For questions about any personal information you choose to email, including correction or deletion requests, use this address. Formal contracting and operator details must be confirmed before any paid engagement.

What happens when you complete a form?

Both inquiry forms on this site open a draft in your email application addressed to the contact above. The site itself does not upload, transmit to a website server, or store the form fields. You must review the prepared email and explicitly press Send in your own email application. A completed form is not confirmation that we received anything. If no email application opens, you can email the contact directly.

If you send an email, its contents and your email address will be processed through your email provider and our Gmail mailbox. Please provide only professional contact details and information relevant to an inquiry; do not include passwords, ID numbers, financial credentials or other sensitive data.

Why we use information

Information you send voluntarily may be used to respond, assess whether a creator or brand collaboration is relevant, prepare a requested proposal and maintain a record of business correspondence. No account is created on this website. A potential introduction to another party would require separate discussion; filling out the form does not enroll you in an automatic public directory.

Storage, disclosure and retention

The website does not maintain its own lead database. Emails you deliberately send may remain in the recipient Gmail account for as long as reasonably needed to handle the inquiry, follow-up correspondence and any legitimate record-keeping obligation. The operator should review and delete stale inquiries. You may request removal by emailing the contact above, subject to obligations to retain particular records.

Messages may be processed by email providers and related infrastructure, including outside your country. Contact details are not intended for sale or public posting. Where a proposed creator-brand introduction would require sharing identifiable information, permission should be obtained first.

Website analytics and hosting

We do not currently include site-specific analytics scripts, advertising pixels or tracking cookies. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may collect technical request data under GitHub's own policies. Clicking external links may take you to other services with their own practices.

Independent status

GameLaunch Bridge is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Any game names are mentioned for factual market context.

Changes

This is a pilot-stage notice. If a proper hosted form service, CRM or analytics provider is introduced later, this notice will need to be reviewed and updated before collection begins.
